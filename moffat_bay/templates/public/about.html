Our lodge

A slower way to meet the island.

By the bay

Our lodge looks toward the protected water of Moffat Bay, close to morning launches and evening shoreline walks.

Made for staying awhile

Comfortable rooms, practical local guidance, and a welcoming common space make it easy to shape each day at your own pace.

Rooted in Joviedsa

We are proud to welcome guests to an island known for its coast, evergreens, and enduring connection to the Salish Sea.
