Island guide

Days shaped by trail, tide, and open water.

Joviedsa Island makes room for a different kind of itinerary. Start close to the lodge, then follow your curiosity.

Hiking

Follow forest paths through cedar shade to high overlooks and quiet stretches of shoreline.

Kayaking

Launch into sheltered coves in the early calm, with the lodge as an easy place to return.

Whale watching

Join a local excursion or scan the water from shore for the island's most remarkable visitors.

Scuba diving

Explore clear-water coves and the changing underwater terrain along the protected coast.
